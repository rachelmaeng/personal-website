ABOUT

Hi, my name is Rachel H Maeng (yes, my middle name is really just H, haha) and I'm years old. I'm a recent graduate from UT Austin with a degree in History.

I started college pre-med, spent a few years as a biology major, and graduated in history. The thread through all of it is that I care more about understanding how things work than practicing inside any one field. I genuinely enjoy digging into archives and figuring out why systems function the way they do, whether that system is a body, an institution, or a marketing funnel, which, as it turns out, is now also my day job!

I cook a lot, because good food is one of life's genuine pleasures. I play the flute, and I fall into Wikipedia spirals far too often.

This site is where I archive the things I'm thinking about, watching, reading, and making.
